About Us

WalletWise is a personal finance platform designed to help users take control of their money through

smart budgeting, expense tracking, and personalized financial insights.

Whether you're a student, professional, or someone working toward financial goals,

WalletWise provides an intuitive and organized way to manage your finances.

Our Features

📊 Expense Tracking

Record and organize daily expenses.

Categorize spending into areas such as food, transportation, shopping, entertainment, and more.

Monitor where your money goes with ease.

📷 Receipt & Photo Scanning

Quickly upload and scan receipts instead of manually entering every expense.

Store expense records digitally for easy access and reference.

🔥 Streak System

Stay motivated by maintaining daily expense-tracking streaks.

Build consistent financial habits and monitor your progress over time.

💰 Personalized Budgeting

Create custom budgets based on your lifestyle and financial goals.

Set spending limits for different categories.

Receive alerts when you're approaching your budget limits.

📈 Spending Summaries & Insights

Identify spending patterns and trends.

Gain insights that help improve financial decision-making.

🎨 Customizable Experience

Choose your preferred currency.

Switch between light and dark mode.

Personalize the app to suit your style and preferences.
